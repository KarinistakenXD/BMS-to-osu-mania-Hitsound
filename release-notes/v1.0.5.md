## Changes

- Similar keysounds now keep a home column. The app uses descriptive names when available and compares the audio for numbered names such as `1.wav`, `_x2.ogg` and `_3.wav`. Clear pitch variants share a column. Uncertain matches stay in separate groups.
- Chords keep one note in the home column and place the extra notes to the right when there is room. If the right side is full, the app uses free columns to the left. Layers beyond the available columns still export as a combined sample.
- Click a note in the preview to see its sample name, timestamp and column and hear it on its own. The inspector also works in standalone previews. A converted note made from several samples lists and plays those layers together.
- **Trim quiet keysound lead-ins** is optional and starts off. It checks the whole sample for a clear attack, ignores small oscillations before it, and keeps uncertain quiet openings. Try it in the converted preview before exporting.

## Export notes

The audio comparison only affects column placement. Samples keep their original pitch. Trimmed exports need FFmpeg. In **Keep original format** mode, edited samples are saved as separate WAV files, leaving the source files intact.
